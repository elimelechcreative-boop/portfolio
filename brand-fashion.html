Brand design · Case study

ROW is a streetwear label from Tel Aviv. I built the whole brand in one system: the mark, the palette, a recurring cast, the lookbook, the reel and the online store.

Contact me
ClientROW Tel Aviv
CategoryStreetwear
ScopeIdentity, cast, reel, store
LanguageHebrew, right to left

A mark that stands upright.

01 · Logo

A condensed wordmark for the label and a boxed R/W monogram for tags, pins and the favicon. Both sit tone on tone on the garments.

Concrete, sand and one orange.

02 · Color and type

The palette comes from the city: rooftop concrete, beach sand, and the orange of a plastic chair on a balcony. Orange is used once per screen, never more.

03 · The cast

Two recurring models on one white set, generated and styled with AI. Every product gets the same light and the same people, so the store reads as one shoot.

The brand, open for business.

04 · Online store

A Hebrew, right-to-left store built on the same system: the drop grid, a lookbook band, a find-the-logo game and the reels, all in one scroll.

ROW Tel Aviv · Store

05 · Drop 01

Six pieces shot flat on concrete for the store, then worn on Tel Aviv rooftops and streets for the lookbook.

ROW is a concept brand. Models, products and photos are AI-generated, and prices on the store are illustrative.

What comes back is a script, the people in it and the prompts to shoot it.

The problem

Every script started from zero.

By hand
  • A blank doc for every new brief
  • Research, strategy and copy done one after another
  • Characters described again for every image tool
  • Feedback lost between rounds
With the tool
  • One brief, handed to the right agents
  • Research and a territory before any copy
  • A character and a prompt for every shot
  • Approved work and feedback remembered per project

Under the hood

How the studio runs.

It looks like one chat with Leo. Behind it are the playbooks he follows and a pipeline that ends in files you can post.

01 · How Leo works

A creative director, not a chatbot.

  • Seven playbooksCampaign launch, single ad, carousel, video ad, revision, market research and a creative review checklist.
  • RoutingAsk for a hook and it goes to Chris. Ask for research and Rachel runs first. Or talk to any agent directly.
  • Review loopLeo checks each draft against the brief and sends it back with notes when it misses.
  • Brand memoryApproved work, feedback and decisions are saved per project, so the next brief starts from them.
  • VisionUpload a logo, a product shot or a reference, and the agents work from what they actually see.

02 · What comes out

From a brief to files.

  • ScriptsHook, body and CTA, scene by scene, with timing.
  • Characters and promptsWho is on screen, and an image prompt for each shot.
  • Final filesPNG, JPG and PDF in 1:1, 4:5, story, banner and A4.
  • Cost controlThree budget modes, and the cost of every run shown live.
  • Hebrew and EnglishWorks in both, right-to-left included.
